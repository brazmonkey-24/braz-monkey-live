# Selected Direction: Signal Zine

## Design Movement
Contemporary risograph editorial design fused with underground science-fiction magazine covers and late-night public-broadcast graphics.

## Core Principles
1. Use deliberate asymmetry: large left-aligned headlines, offset cards, and diagonal signal lines rather than centered landing-page symmetry.
2. Treat texture as information: screen-print grain, thin rules, scanline bands, and small metadata labels make every section feel transmitted rather than decorated.
3. Keep the palette disciplined: near-black, paper white, acid yellow, hot pink, and a restrained signal red.
4. Make the mascot and the weekly rhythm the emotional anchors; every interaction should feel like tuning into a recurring transmission.

## Color Philosophy
Near-black creates the unknown space Braz Monkey landed in. Acid yellow is the primary signal and feels energetic without becoming generic tech blue. Hot pink marks the human weirdness and humor of the character. Paper white keeps long-form copy readable, while signal red is reserved for live or urgent states.

## Layout Paradigm
A broadcast sheet: fixed utility rail and wide hero field, then staggered editorial modules that alternate between dense information and generous dark breathing room. Cards align to a shared left signal line but break the grid with offset numbers, vertical labels, and occasional full-bleed art.

## Signature Elements
- A repeating top transmission ticker with diamond signal separators.
- Small monospace metadata labels such as `// ORIGIN FILE: UNKNOWN` and numbered transmission cards.
- Rough halftone artwork framed by hairline rules and asymmetric acid-yellow blocks.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like tuning equipment: buttons snap with a slight physical press, filters switch the active signal immediately, and hover states reveal a hot-pink frequency line rather than a soft generic glow. Links are explicit and directional.

## Animation
Use short, purposeful motion under 300ms for controls. Let the ticker drift continuously, let hero artwork breathe with a slow parallax shift, and reveal sections with a restrained upward slide. Respect reduced-motion preferences by freezing the ticker and removing nonessential transforms.

## Typography System
Display: `Bebas Neue` for compressed, poster-scale headlines. Body: `IBM Plex Mono` for readable broadcast copy and metadata. Use uppercase display headings with tight tracking; use sentence case only for longer descriptions. Metadata is 10–12px with wide tracking and acid-yellow color.

## Brand Essence
Braz Monkey is a weekly alien transmission for curious people who like sharp observations, cosmic predictions, and old-school records without polished influencer energy. Personality: **curious, dry, magnetic**.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are blunt and slightly mysterious. CTAs are commands that imply a signal or discovery. Microcopy is dry, observant, and never generic.

Example lines:
- “NO PAST. JUST SIGNAL.”
- “TUNE IN BEFORE THE PLANET CHANGES CHANNEL.”

## Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact custom-feeling wordmark in uppercase condensed lettering with a broken scanline underline, paired with a symbol-only alien-broadcast mark for the header and favicon.

## Signature Brand Color
**Signal Yellow — `#f5e642`**, an ownable acid yellow that reads as both warning stripe and optimistic transmission beacon.

## Style Decisions
- Preserve the original Braz Monkey black/yellow/pink identity, but elevate it with generated editorial artwork and clearer responsive hierarchy.
- The permanent version will prioritize a single strong homepage with anchor navigation, keeping the restored story, episodes, about, and community content discoverable in the same broadcast flow.
- Use generated visuals in prominent areas; do not reuse one image in multiple sections.
